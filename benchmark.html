Data fidelity — the difference that isn't speed

Wikipedia rowspans the Headquarters column across runs of rows sharing a country. Flat text extraction drops the spanned cell, so 13 of 50 companies come back with no country and nothing signals the loss. Bridge resolves the table to a real occupancy grid and repeats the value into every row it covers. This is a wrong answer versus a right one, not a slow answer versus a fast one.
